# Neon Solitude

## Collection direction

Quiet, isolated nighttime locations rendered with deep navy and charcoal
surroundings, restrained cyan and magenta illumination, realistic wet surfaces,
and subtle fog. The collection should feel contemplative rather than crowded or
aggressively cyberpunk.

Every design requires separate lock-screen and home-screen compositions. Lock
screens preserve clear space for the clock and notifications. Home screens keep
important details away from common icon and widget areas.
