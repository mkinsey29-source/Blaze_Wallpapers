# 001 — Solitary Station

## Status

First-pass collection concept for real-device evaluation. Not yet approved as a
commercial release asset.

## Creation

- Created: 2026-09-15
- Method: OpenAI built-in image generation, followed by deterministic resizing
  and light output sharpening for the 1440 x 3120 device exports.
- Original source dimensions: 851 x 1848 pixels
- Original subject: an unbranded, deserted modern gas station in a forested
  mountain setting at night, with wet pavement, fog, cyan illumination, and a
  restrained magenta accent.
- Prohibited elements specified during creation: people, vehicles, brands,
  logos, readable signs, watermarks, UI, clocks, and copyrighted characters.

## Variants

- Lock screen: larger station and reflections in the lower portion, with an
  intentionally dark clock and notification region.
- Home screen: station positioned higher and slightly smaller to provide calmer
  space behind icons and widgets.

## Device exports

- `exports/1440x3120/neon-solitude-001-solitary-station-lock-1440x3120.webp`
- `exports/1440x3120/neon-solitude-001-solitary-station-home-1440x3120.webp`

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Inspect both versions on the Galaxy S25 Ultra at native resolution.
