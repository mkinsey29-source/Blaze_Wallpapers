# 002 — Last Bus

## Status

First-pass collection concept for real-device evaluation. Not yet approved as a
commercial release asset.

## Creation

- Created: 2026-09-15
- Method: OpenAI built-in image generation, followed by deterministic resizing
  and light output sharpening for the 1440 x 3120 device exports.
- Original source dimensions: 851 x 1848 pixels
- Original subject: an empty modern glass bus shelter beside a wet forest road
  at night, illuminated by restrained cyan and amber light in rain and mist.
- Prohibited elements specified during creation: people, vehicles, brands,
  advertisements, logos, readable timetables, route numbers, watermarks, UI,
  clocks, and copyrighted characters.

## Variants

- Lock screen: shelter and reflective road create the focal area in the lower
  portion while the upper sky remains dark for the clock and notifications.
- Home screen: shelter sits slightly higher and smaller, with a calmer central
  field behind icons and widgets.
