# 003 — Rooftop Silence

## Status

First-pass collection concept for real-device evaluation. Not yet approved as a
commercial release asset.

## Creation

- Created: 2026-09-15
- Method: OpenAI built-in image generation, followed by deterministic resizing
  and light output sharpening for the 1440 x 3120 device exports.
- Original source dimensions: 851 x 1848 pixels
- Original subject: an empty rain-wet rooftop overlooking a misty waterfront
  city after midnight, with one metal chair, restrained utility lighting,
  planters, ventilation structures, puddles, and a distant original skyline.
- Prohibited elements specified during creation: people, famous landmarks,
  brands, billboards, logos, readable signs, watermarks, UI, clocks, and
  copyrighted buildings or characters.

## Variants

- Lock screen: chair, ledge, and skyline form the lower focal grouping while the
  upper sky remains dark for the clock and notifications.
- Home screen: rooftop grouping sits slightly higher and smaller, with reduced
  skyline and reflection dominance behind icons and widgets.
