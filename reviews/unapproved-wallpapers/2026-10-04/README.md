## Integrity and review decisions

[Machine-readable inventory](inventory.json) retains original filenames, byte sizes, SHA-256 for native PNGs, and exact Git blob hashes for every archived image. Recovery sources are pinned to a repository commit. No artwork bytes have been edited.

When deciding, identify the stable design label or recovery scene and the orientation. Record approval, revision request, rejection, or supersession explicitly. Supersession is a future decision here; the presence of newer designs does not erase earlier review states.
