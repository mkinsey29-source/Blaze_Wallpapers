# Design insights from the gallery references

## What appears consistently attractive

The boards span very different tastes, but the successful-looking images share
several structural traits:

- One immediately readable focal subject or silhouette.
- Strong vertical composition made specifically for a phone rather than a crop
  from a landscape image.
- A limited dominant palette with one high-impact accent color.
- Clear depth: foreground framing, a middle subject, and a distant atmosphere.
- Negative space around the upper clock region and calmer areas behind icons.
- Familiar subjects presented with heightened lighting, scale, color, or mood.
- Dark OLED-friendly scenes and light minimal scenes both have clear audiences.
- Roads, coastlines, bridges, railways, windows, and beams of light provide
  natural visual paths through a tall screen.

## Recommended Blaze collection families

These are original category directions derived from broad market signals, not
copies of individual reference images:

1. **Neon Solitude** — rainy night locations, quiet cities, restrained neon.
2. **Afterglow Roads** — sunset highways, coastlines, overlooks, and long paths.
3. **Quiet Cosmos** — celestial scale, sparse astronauts, moons, and deep space.
4. **Wild Presence** — original cinematic wildlife portraits and silhouettes.
5. **Soft Minimal** — pale backgrounds, one centered object, gentle shadows.
6. **Ink & Form** — monochrome abstraction, flowing lines, paper and stone.
7. **Botanical Calm** — flowers, leaves, misty forests, and subtle seasonal color.
8. **Painted Worlds** — stylized original landscapes with controlled palettes.

Seasonal packs can borrow a collection's established language instead of
becoming a disconnected set of holiday clip art.

## Gallery implications

- Lead with one large featured image, then switch to a dense two- or three-column
  image grid as the catalog grows.
- Organize primarily by named collection; let users move between collections
  without assigning public titles to individual pictures.
- Treat ordinary static wallpaper as the unlabeled default.
- Show format badges only for non-default experiences such as `PARALLAX` or
  `MOTION`.
- Keep controls off the image except for a compact favorite affordance.
- Use full-bleed thumbnails with consistent corner radii and very little text.
- Preserve the current direct preview-to-Home/Lock/Both application flow.

## Blaze quality lift

Blaze should use the popular visual ideas at a higher execution standard:

- Original art direction and independently created scenes.
- Separate home- and lock-optimized compositions.
- Device-ready QHD/4K exports with no forced customer cropping.
- Physically coherent geometry, reflections, anatomy, perspective, and lighting.
- No generation artifacts, fake writing, compression damage, banding, or blurry
  detail when inspected at full size.
- Consistent color grading and quality within each collection.
- Thumbnail crops that accurately represent the delivered wallpaper.
- Real-device checks on the Galaxy S25 Ultra, Galaxy S10, and a midrange device.

## Rights boundary

Do not reproduce or imitate recognizable anime/game characters, franchise
costumes, logos, branded products, famous artwork, living artists' signatures,
or reference compositions closely enough to function as substitutes. Extract
only general design principles and create every production brief from scratch.
