# Wallpaper gallery inspiration

The files in `gallery-boards/` are user-provided visual reference boards for
studying broad wallpaper-market preferences. They are **not Blaze assets** and
must never be packaged into the app, published as wallpapers, traced, edited
into saleable work, or used as direct image-generation inputs.

Some boards contain recognizable characters, brands, artists' work,
photographs, and other third-party material. Their presence here does not imply
a commercial license or ownership. They exist only to communicate high-level
preferences such as palette, contrast, subject scale, negative space,
composition, gallery density, and potential category breadth.

Production artwork must be independently created from original briefs and pass
the rights and quality checks in `DESIGN_INSIGHTS.md`.
