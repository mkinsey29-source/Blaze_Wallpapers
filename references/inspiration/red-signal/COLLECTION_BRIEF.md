# Red Signal — collection direction

`Red Signal` is a working customer-facing collection name for one of Blaze's
flagship visual directions. It can be renamed before release.

## Core identity

- Graphic neo-punk and cybernetic portraiture with the force of a screen-printed
  concert poster or underground comic cover.
- A deliberately restricted palette: signal red, dense black, bone white, and
  rare hazard-yellow accents.
- Coarse halftone, ink breakup, paper grain, and hard-edged shadows used as
  intentional design elements rather than filters applied at the end.
- One immediately readable original subject or silhouette, framed by targets,
  concentric circles, angular bursts, torn-poster shapes, or abstract signal
  graphics.
- Rebellious and futuristic without depending on an existing franchise,
  character, band, logo, uniform, or recognizable product.

## Original subject directions

Good starting points include masked couriers, rogue radio operators, street
musicians, biomechanical sentinels, anonymous rebels, and invented machines.
Each subject needs an original silhouette, face covering, clothing system,
hardware, insignia, and pose. Do not reproduce the references' masks,
headphones, spikes, costumes, symbols, equipment, or compositions.

## Phone-first composition

- Build vertically at the target device ratio; do not crop a poster after the
  fact.
- Reserve a calmer upper clock zone in the lock-screen composition.
- Keep the home-screen composition readable behind icons by moving fine facial
  detail and noisy halftone away from the busiest icon regions.
- Deliver a coordinated lock/home pair rather than one image stretched or
  shifted for both uses.
- Prefer a strong chest-up portrait or full-height silhouette with enough open
  background to preserve impact on small screens.

## Quality lift

- Crisp silhouettes and controlled halftone at QHD/4K export size.
- Coherent anatomy, clothing construction, cables, fasteners, and invented
  hardware.
- Clean color separations with rich blacks and reds that survive OLED display,
  compression, and thumbnail reduction.
- No fake microtext, accidental letters, copied patches, existing logos, noisy
  AI detail, or arbitrary technology shapes.
- Texture should remain legible without making the wallpaper appear blurry or
  low resolution.

## Parallax potential

This direction is a strong future parallax collection. A production piece can
be authored as three restrained depth planes:

1. Background target, rays, or poster field.
2. Main figure and large equipment silhouette.
3. Foreground ink flecks, torn edges, cables, or signal marks.

Movement should be subtle enough to retain the printed-poster character. The
ordinary version remains unlabeled; only the enhanced version receives a
`PARALLAX` or `MOTION` type label in the app.

## Catalog treatment

Use `Red Signal` as the collection/category name. Individual wallpapers do not
need public titles. This keeps the gallery visual and avoids naming dozens of
ordinary images while still giving customers a memorable style to browse.

## Rights boundary

The JPGs in this folder are user-provided third-party visual references only.
They must not ship in the app, be sold, traced, edited into production assets,
or be used as direct image-generation inputs. Production work must follow this
independent written brief and be reviewed for recognizable characters,
costumes, masks, symbols, logos, and composition overlap.
